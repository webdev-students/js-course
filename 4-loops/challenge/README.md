# Challenge: Loop practice

**Time:** about 25 min

Three small loop jobs in one `script.js`:

1. **FizzBuzz** — print the numbers 1 to 15, but print `Fizz` instead of numbers divisible by 3, `Buzz` for numbers divisible by 5, and `FizzBuzz` for numbers divisible by both.
2. **Reverse a word** — turn `'London'` into `'nodnoL'` with a loop (count down from the last position).
3. **Price list** — print a price list from $10 to $50 in steps of $10, and stop early (with `break`) at the first price over a budget of $35.

**What you should see in the Console:**

```text
1
2
Fizz
4
Buzz
Fizz
7
8
Fizz
Buzz
11
Fizz
13
14
FizzBuzz
nodnoL
$10
$20
$30
$40 is over budget — stopping.
```
